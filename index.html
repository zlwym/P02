Branding Elements

Company Details

Oak's Archive is a collector's store, housing a range of books, zines, catalogues, postcards, prints, and stationary items which provoke serene naturalistic imagery. The store draws in minimalists, alongside editorial and literary lovers with an appreciation for natural aesthetics as represented in the products Oak's Archive sells. The simple yet high quality items draw customers in, offering functionality that blends seamlessly in everyday life while bringing in elements of the outdoors to the comforts of one's home.

Persona

Overview

Jordan is a thirty year-old, full-time naturalist journalist with a passion for old fashion aesthetics, literature and publishing. His walls are lined bookshelves filled with botany and nature books, while his coffee table often features a seasonal book with ocean photography. Jordan enjoys sending postcards to his family overseas with cover designs that match his style.

Behaviour

He often looks for these niche books at stores that match his interest on both a content and visual level. He also purchases postcards with nice imagery to fill up his letter-writing storage. However, Jordan faces trouble at times because many books stores feature mainstream books and products, rather than topics and styles that appeal to him. Because of this, he turns to online stores to hunt down products, which also proves to be challenging because even online stores do not offer many options, causing him to look through many stores.

Goals

  1. Discovering books, publishing material, and writing stationary that fit within his niche aesthetics and hobbies
  2. Purchasing books and stationary efficiently by discovering a company that sells many products that match his aesthetic in one site

Weaknesses

  1. Stores with too many mainstream and popularized books and designs that does not fit his interest nor match his home aesthetic
  2. Sites that are difficult to navigate when trying to purchase items
