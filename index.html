Interactive Elements

In-text link

CSS selector:

.in-text-link

Sample code:

<a class="in-text-link" href = "https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dog_food">dog food</a>

Rendered element:

dog food

Navigation Link

CSS selector:

.button-nav

Sample code:

<a href="#" class="button">Default Button</a>

Rendered element:

Default Button

p

Dogs have been a notable animal since early human history, appearing in ancient hieroglyphics and art pieces.</p>

Branding

Violet's Pet Shop offers a curated selection of high-quality, locally sourced dog and cat food, toys, accessories, and grooming supplies. The shop prioritizes pet health and wellbeing, selling products from local brands to increase sustainability.

To potential customers, the company is a passionate, knowledgable entity that loves animals and helping people care for theirs. It is approachable, trustworthy, and enthusiastic.

The company might entice someone to purchase a product or service because they are drawn in by the shop's commitment to quality, locally sourced goods, pet health and wellbeing, and thoughtfully curated product offerings. The shop has genuine care for pets and makes the shopping experience personable and reliable.
